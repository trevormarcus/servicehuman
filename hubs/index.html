What a HUB is

24/7 • Human-first • Accountable

A Service Human HUB is a always-open, calm, well-run space where people can get immediate help without judgment, hoops, or performative bureaucracy. Think: the reliability of an airport + the warmth of a community center + the transparency of a public ledger.

What happens inside

Not a vague promise — a real workflow.

  • Front Desk: quick intake, triage, and calm navigation.
  • Basics: water, food, hygiene kits, showers, laundry, charging, rest space.
  • Stabilization: safe rooms, crisis de-escalation, medical referrals.
  • Case Support: ID/birth certificate help, benefits, housing placement support.
  • Forward Motion: job readiness, community partners, and follow-up.

Design principles

We build HUBs the way great systems are built: clear standards, repeatable operations, and receipts.

  • 24/7 means 24/7. No “come back tomorrow.”
  • Safety without intimidation.
  • Dignity without paternalism.
  • Transparency without spin.
  • Scalable across major metros.

Rollout model

How we go from “one HUB” to a national backbone.

  • Phase 1: Prototype (Tampa) — prove operations, prove outcomes.
  • Phase 2: Regional scale — copy/paste the playbook into 3–5 metros.
  • Phase 3: National network — shared standards, local partners, unified visibility.

How this ties into LIVE

The HUB page is the “vision.” The LIVE page is the “heartbeat.” Every HUB can have its own public panel: funds in, funds out, people served, and goals.

  • Public numbers → public trust
  • Transparency → pressure to perform
  • Receipts → less room for corruption
