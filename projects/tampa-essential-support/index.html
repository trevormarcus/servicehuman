What this project funds

This campaign funds structured, immediate support for individuals facing housing instability in Tampa Bay. The purpose is stabilization—fast, practical, and measurable—while connecting individuals to longer-term pathways.

Use of funds (high-level)

  • Essential supplies: hygiene items, basic necessities, and rapid-response support needs
  • Food access: immediate nourishment support when needed for stabilization
  • Transportation assistance: connecting individuals to services, appointments, and intake locations
  • Intake coordination: documented service connection steps (case coordination notes)
  • Local deployment costs: verified Tampa Bay operational expenses directly tied to delivery

What success looks like

  • 500 Essential Support packages delivered (or as many as fully funded)
  • Documented distribution totals and allocation summaries
  • Transparent project closeout summary at campaign end

FAQ

Is this a general donation fund?

No. This is a designated project. Funds attributed to this campaign are allocated specifically to Tampa-based essential support operations.

How is impact measured?

We track funded package count, distribution totals, and high-level allocation categories. A campaign closeout summary is published at the end of the window.

Can my company sponsor or match this campaign?

Yes. This campaign is structured for workplace giving platforms and corporate reporting requirements. We can align custom reporting fields if needed.

What happens if the campaign exceeds the goal?

Additional funds extend the package count and/or the campaign scope within the same Tampa-based project definition, with reporting updated accordingly.
