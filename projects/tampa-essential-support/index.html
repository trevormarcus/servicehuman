Project Summary

Tampa Essential Support Campaign (2026 Q1) was originally created as a workplace-giving compatible initiative designed to fund Essential Support Packages within the Tampa Bay region.

  • Campaign Window: February 22, 2026 – May 31, 2026
  • Funding Goal: $12,500
  • Planned Deliverables: 500 Essential Support Packages

  • Disbursements: $0

What this project was designed to fund

This campaign was designed to fund structured, immediate support for individuals facing housing instability in Tampa Bay. The intended purpose was stabilization through essential supplies, food access, transportation assistance, intake coordination, and local deployment support.

Planned use of funds

  • Essential supplies: hygiene items, basic necessities, and rapid-response support needs
  • Food access: immediate nourishment support when needed for stabilization
  • Transportation assistance: connecting individuals to services, appointments, and intake locations
  • Intake coordination: documented service connection steps
  • Local deployment costs: verified Tampa Bay operational expenses directly tied to delivery

Final project outcome

  • No designated funding was received.
  • No Essential Support Packages were deployed under this campaign.
  • No Benevity donations, grants, or disbursements occurred.
  • The campaign completed its scheduled window and is preserved as a historical project record.
