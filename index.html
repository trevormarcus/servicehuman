About Service Human

Service Human, Inc. exists for one purpose: to help people. We do not exist to make a profit, to chase status, or to build an empire. We exist to make it easier for one human being to help another.

  • People first. We help individuals without conditions or labels.
  • No executive bloat. No excess salaries.
  • Transparency. Donors can see how funds are allocated.
  • Collaboration. We work with governments, but we are not controlled by them.

Reporting Period: January 1–31, 2025
Record Type: Administrative and Strategic Activities Summary
Purpose: Documentation of organizational planning and foundational development

January 2025 was dedicated to foundational planning, structural clarification, and early-stage development of Service Human, Inc.’s long-term mission framework. Activities during this period focused on establishing direction, defining operational principles, and evaluating sustainable approaches to building a humanitarian organization grounded in transparency and ethical integrity.

Matters Discussed

Mission Definition and Guiding Principles

  • Continued refinement of Service Human’s core purpose: providing direct support to human beings without discrimination or profit-driven motives.
  • Emphasis placed on avoiding traditional nonprofit inefficiencies, excessive administrative overhead, and executive compensation structures inconsistent with the organization’s philosophy.
  • Reinforced commitment to radical transparency as a defining operational value.

Organizational Structure and Sustainability

  • Explored models for building a lean organizational structure capable of scaling responsibly over time.
  • Discussed the importance of protecting the mission from burnout, overextension, and reliance on performative or non-committal support.
  • Considered strategies for setting clear expectations around volunteer involvement and advisory participation.

Advisory and Skill-Based Support Considerations

  • Evaluated the potential role of individuals with financial, strategic, or entrepreneurial experience to contribute in advisory or consultative capacities.
  • Focused on identifying contributions based on practical action and aligned values rather than titles or symbolic involvement.

Public Presence and Communication Strategy

  • Reviewed approaches for introducing Service Human publicly in a way that prioritizes clarity over promotion.
  • Emphasis placed on measured communication, mission education, and avoiding exaggerated claims or urgency-based messaging.
  • Recognized the importance of building credibility through documented action rather than visibility alone.

Early Infrastructure and Tooling

  • Explored low-cost and free tools to support early-stage operations, digital presence, and organizational documentation.
  • Considered equipment and logistical resources that may support future field documentation and outreach activities, though no operational deployment occurred during this period.

Actions Taken

  • No formal resolutions, financial disbursements, or program launches occurred during this reporting period.
  • Work remained focused on planning, mission clarification, and preparatory development.

Status and Outlook

January 2025 served as an early-stage alignment period, ensuring that future growth would be rooted in clearly defined values, ethical guardrails, and operational discipline. The groundwork laid during this period informed subsequent structural, transparency, and documentation efforts later in the year.

This record is maintained as part of Service Human, Inc.’s official organizational documentation.

Reporting Period: December 1–31, 2025
Record Type: Administrative and Strategic Activities Summary
Purpose: Documentation of organizational planning and governance activity

December 2025 was dedicated to organizational planning, strategic development, and infrastructure preparation in support of Service Human, Inc.’s charitable mission. Activities during this period were administrative and developmental in nature and did not involve the execution of direct services or the disbursement of funds.

Matters Discussed

  • Mission governance: reaffirmed charitable purpose and guiding principles; emphasized mission integrity.
  • Transparency: discussed preliminary frameworks for financial transparency, donor accountability, and public reporting.
  • Infrastructure: continued development of digital and administrative systems to support scalability and audit readiness.
  • Program planning: advanced conceptual planning for Service Human HUB initiatives; no final timelines or locations set.
  • Funding/partnerships: explored ethical models and safeguards to prevent conflicts of interest and mission drift.
  • Public narrative: reviewed messaging for accuracy, restraint, and alignment with transparency commitments.

Actions Taken

  • No formal resolutions, votes, or binding commitments were made during this period.
  • Work remained focused on planning, documentation, internal review, and preparatory development.
