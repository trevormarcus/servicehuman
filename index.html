About Service Human

Service Human, Inc. exists for one purpose: to help people. We do not exist to make a profit, to chase status, or to build an empire. We exist to make it easier for one human being to help another.

  • People first. We help individuals without conditions or labels.
  • No executive bloat. No excess salaries.
  • Transparency. Donors can see how funds are allocated.
  • Collaboration. We work with governments, but we are not controlled by them.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is Service Human, Inc.?

A nonprofit built to make it easier for one human being to help another—directly, transparently, and at scale.

Where does the money go?

Primarily to direct human support. A small portion sustains the infrastructure required to deliver that support consistently and transparently. See it live.

How is this different from other charities?

Service Human is designed around visibility. You don’t have to trust claims — you can verify outcomes in real time.

What is a Service Human HUB?

A Service Human HUB is a local support center providing stabilization, coordination, and direct pathways to resources.

Do you guarantee outcomes or returns?

No. This is human support infrastructure &emdash; not an investment product. The goal is participation, visibility, and consistent delivery of help.
