What Service Human does

Service Human exists to make it easier for one human being to help another — directly, transparently, and at scale.

Direct Support

Immediate human assistance for essential needs like food, hygiene, transportation, stabilization, and emergency support.

Service Human HUBs

Future 24/7 support centers designed around dignity, safety, basic needs, partner services, and practical pathways forward.

LIVE Transparency

Public-facing reporting so donors and communities can see funds, activity, and impact without relying on vague claims.

Why this exists

Too many people fall through the cracks because support systems are fragmented, slow, difficult to access, or impossible to understand.

Service Human is built around a simple belief: support should be based on being human.

The goal is not charity theater. The goal is practical infrastructure: clear intake, direct support, trusted partners, and visible accountability.

The HUB vision

A Service Human HUB is envisioned as an always-open support center where people can stabilize, reset, and connect to real pathways forward.

  • Showers, laundry, hygiene, and essentials
  • Charging, lockers, rest space, and safe navigation
  • Partner services for health, legal, workforce, and housing pathways
  • Transparent reporting tied to local activity and outcomes

Frequently Asked Questions

What is Service Human, Inc.?

Service Human, Inc. is a 501(c)(3) nonprofit built to make it easier for one human being to help another directly, transparently, and at scale.

Where does the money go?

Primarily to direct human support. A small portion sustains the infrastructure required to deliver that support consistently and transparently.

What is a Service Human HUB?

A Service Human HUB is a future local support center providing stabilization, coordination, basic needs access, and direct pathways to resources.

How is Service Human different?

Service Human is designed around visibility. Donors and communities should be able to verify activity, allocation, and outcomes instead of relying only on claims.

Does Service Human guarantee outcomes or returns?

No. Service Human is human support infrastructure, not an investment product. The goal is participation, visibility, and consistent delivery of help.
