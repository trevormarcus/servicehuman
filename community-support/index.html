What Service Human HUBs do

Stabilization

  • Basic needs support (food access, hygiene supplies, clothing resources)
  • Crisis navigation and referrals
  • Short-term planning to reduce immediate risk

Resource connection

  • Trusted partner referrals (medical, housing, workforce, counseling)
  • Document and appointment readiness help (non-legal)
  • Community navigation and follow-ups

How we handle legal & documentation questions

Service Human does not provide legal advice or strategy. We connect people to qualified professionals, including licensed attorneys and accredited nonprofit providers, and we share general information so people can access accurate, lawful guidance.

Responsibility & compliance

  • We do not interfere with law enforcement activity.
  • We do not conceal individuals or obstruct investigations.
  • We maintain a neutral humanitarian stance focused on stability and lawful resource access.

Privacy & trust

  • We collect minimal information needed to connect someone with services.
  • We do not require immigration status unless a specific partner program requires it.
  • Staff are trained in confidentiality and ethical information handling.

If you’re a partner organization: we can align intake fields to your requirements while keeping our default intake minimal.

FAQ

Who can use Service Human HUB services?

Anyone facing vulnerability who needs stabilization and referral support. Services vary by HUB capacity and local partners.

Do you provide immigration or legal advice?

No. We provide general information and connect people to qualified legal and accredited nonprofit providers.

Do you collect immigration status or personal documentation?

We collect minimal information by default. We do not require immigration status unless a specific partner program requires it.

Is this a “sanctuary” location?

We don’t use political labels. We are a neutral service organization focused on lawful support pathways and community stability.

How can organizations partner or refer people?

Email trevor@servicehumaninc.org with your referral workflow, eligibility rules, and the data fields you require. We’ll align intake to your program while keeping our default intake minimal.
