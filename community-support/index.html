A Service Human Hub Initiative

Service Human Hubs provide stabilization, resource connection, and pathways to lawful support services for individuals and families facing vulnerability.

Human Dignity Legal Compliance Community Stability

We serve people. We do not take political positions.

What Service Human Hubs Do

  • Basic Needs: food access, hygiene supplies, clothing resources, emergency essentials
  • Wellness: mental health first response and referrals, trauma-informed support, crisis navigation
  • Empowerment: language learning, financial literacy, workforce readiness, community navigation

How We Handle Legal & Documentation Questions

Service Human does not provide legal advice or strategy. We connect people to qualified professionals, including licensed attorneys and accredited nonprofit providers.

We share general information and trusted referrals so people can access accurate, lawful guidance.

Responsibility & Compliance

  • We do not interfere with law enforcement activity.
  • We do not conceal individuals or obstruct investigations.
  • We maintain a neutral humanitarian stance focused on stability and lawful resource access.

Privacy & Trust

  • We collect minimal information needed to connect someone with services.
  • We do not require immigration status unless a specific partner program requires it.
  • Staff are trained in confidentiality and ethical information handling.

FAQ

Who can use Service Human Hub services?

Anyone in the community who is facing hardship or needs help navigating support resources.

Do you provide immigration or legal advice?

No. We share general information and connect people to licensed attorneys and accredited nonprofit legal service providers.

Do you collect immigration status or personal documentation?

We collect minimal information needed to connect someone with services. We do not require immigration status unless a specific partner program requires it.

Is this a “sanctuary” location?

No. Service Human Hubs are humanitarian support and referral centers. We do not interfere with law enforcement activity.

How can organizations partner or refer people?

Partners can coordinate referrals, workshops led by qualified professionals, and resource connections aligned with community stability and lawful support pathways.
