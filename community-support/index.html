How support works

1. Intake

A person reaches out directly or is referred by a partner organization. Service Human gathers only the minimum information needed to understand the situation.

2. Stabilization

Immediate needs are identified and stabilized where possible through direct essentials support, practical guidance, and calm navigation.

3. Resource Connection

Individuals are connected to trusted partner organizations, licensed professionals, and lawful pathways for longer-term stability.

What support can look like

Stabilization

  • Food access
  • Hygiene support
  • Essential supplies
  • Clothing resources
  • Crisis navigation and referrals
  • Short-term planning to reduce immediate risk

Resource Connection

  • Housing pathways
  • Workforce resources
  • Medical referrals
  • Counseling referrals
  • Community partnerships
  • Document and appointment readiness support

Neutral. Lawful. Human.

Service Human does not provide legal advice or legal strategy.

Service Human connects people to qualified professionals, including licensed attorneys and accredited nonprofit providers, while sharing general information so people can access accurate and lawful guidance.

Responsibility & compliance

  • We do not interfere with law enforcement activity.
  • We do not conceal individuals or obstruct investigations.
  • We maintain a neutral humanitarian stance focused on stability and lawful resource access.

Privacy & trust

  • We collect minimal information needed to connect someone with services.
  • We do not require immigration status unless a specific partner program requires it.
  • Staff and partners should follow confidentiality and ethical information-handling practices.

If you are a partner organization, intake fields can be aligned to your requirements while keeping Service Human’s default intake minimal.

Frequently Asked Questions

Who can use Service Human HUB services?

Anyone facing vulnerability who needs stabilization and referral support. Services vary by HUB capacity and local partners.

Do you provide immigration or legal advice?

No. Service Human provides general information and connects people to qualified legal and accredited nonprofit providers when legal support is needed.

Do you collect immigration status or personal documentation?

Service Human collects minimal information by default. Immigration status is not required unless a specific partner program requires it.

Is this a “sanctuary” location?

Service Human does not use political labels. It is a neutral service organization focused on lawful support pathways and community stability.

How can organizations partner or refer people?

Email info@servicehumaninc.org with your referral workflow, eligibility rules, and required data fields.
