What Community Support Means Here

What we do

  • Basic Needs: food access, hygiene supplies, clothing resources, emergency essentials
  • Wellness: crisis navigation, trauma-informed support, referrals to licensed providers
  • Stability: benefits navigation, housing pathways, workforce readiness connections
  • Documentation support: help understanding required forms and connecting to qualified services

What we do not do

  • We do not provide legal advice or legal strategy.
  • We do not interfere with law enforcement activity.
  • We do not conceal individuals or obstruct investigations.
  • We do not ask people to disclose sensitive status unless a specific program requires it.

Our role is simple: help people stabilize and connect with lawful, appropriate resources — quickly and respectfully.

Stabilization

  • Emergency essentials
  • Short-term safety planning
  • Crisis navigation and warm handoffs

Resource Connection

  • Food and hygiene access
  • Community partner referrals
  • Transportation and appointment coordination (when available)

Pathways to Lawful Support

  • Referrals to licensed attorneys and accredited providers
  • Trusted nonprofit and community programs
  • General info on how to access official resources

Privacy & Trust

  • Minimal information collection
  • Confidential handling and ethical training
  • We focus on needs, not labels

FAQ

Who can use Service Human Hub services?

Anyone seeking stabilization or connection to lawful support resources. We focus on needs and safety first.

Do you provide immigration or legal advice?

No. We do not provide legal advice or strategy. We connect people to qualified professionals, including licensed attorneys and accredited nonprofit providers, and we share general information about how to access official resources.

Do you collect immigration status or personal documentation?

We collect minimal information needed to connect someone with services. We do not require immigration status unless a specific partner program requires it, and we aim to keep intake respectful and limited.

Is this a “sanctuary” location?

Service Human is a neutral humanitarian organization focused on stabilization and resource access. We do not interfere with law enforcement activity, and we do not take political positions.

How can organizations partner or refer people?

Use the Partner / Refer section above. We’ll confirm scope, capacity, referral criteria, and privacy expectations so referrals stay lawful, clean, and effective.
