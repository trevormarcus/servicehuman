Why Verification Exists

Most nonprofits ask you to trust them. Service Human is built so you don’t have to.

Verification is the backbone of our platform. It ensures that every dollar, every partner, and every impact claim can be independently confirmed through financial institutions, corporate giving networks, and public records.

This is not marketing. This is infrastructure.

What Is Being Verified?

  • Our legal identity – State, federal, and nonprofit registration
  • Our banking – Institutional financial verification
  • Our eligibility – Corporate and workplace giving platforms
  • Our financial flows – Where money enters and where it goes
  • Our impact – Programs, grants, and deployments

These layers form a continuous chain of trust — not a single point of failure.

Our Verification Networks

Service Human is registered, vetted, and monitored inside the same networks used by the world’s largest employers, foundations, and financial institutions.

Candid (GuideStar)

Confirms our nonprofit identity, leadership, mission, and IRS-recognized status. This is the public record used by foundations, journalists, and donors.

Benevity

The corporate giving platform used by companies like Microsoft, Apple, Google, Disney, and thousands more. Benevity verifies our banking, compliance, and eligibility for employee donations and matching.

Bonterra

Powers workplace giving, grants management, and corporate philanthropy. Bonterra verifies nonprofits before allowing them into corporate donor pipelines.

Blackbaud

The global nonprofit financial and fundraising infrastructure used by universities, hospitals, and large foundations. Blackbaud ensures that organizations receiving funds are legitimate, traceable, and compliant.

What This Means for Donors

When you give to Service Human, your donation is not just accepted — it is validated by multiple independent systems.

  • Your employer can match it
  • Your payroll system can verify it
  • Your foundation can audit it
  • Your accountant can trace it

This eliminates the two biggest risks in philanthropy: misuse of funds and lack of accountability.

Why Some Organizations Avoid Verification

Verification requires disclosure. It requires consistency. It requires numbers that withstand scrutiny.

Many organizations choose opacity because it allows inefficiency, excessive overhead, and vague impact claims to survive.

Service Human was built for the opposite.
