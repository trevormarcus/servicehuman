Core What verification means

Verification at Service Human means three things: (1) money is real, (2) impact is real, and (3) claims are provable. This is not a “trust us” model — it’s a “show me” model.

Most organizations ask for trust. Service Human replaces trust with visibility.
Why Why this exists

The nonprofit world often relies on narratives, not numbers. Donors are asked to believe impact happened. Service Human is built so that impact is observable — not assumed.

  • Fraud hides in darkness.
  • Waste hides in complexity.
  • Trust collapses when numbers can’t be seen.
System The four verification layers

1) Identity Verification

Confirms that an entity is real, legitimate, and accountable.

  • Government registries / formation records
  • IRS / nonprofit status where applicable
  • Authorized officers & ownership / control checks

2) Financial Verification

Confirms that money is real and movements are traceable.

  • Banking rails (ACH / wire / card processing)
  • Receipts, timestamps, and attribution
  • Funds-in / funds-out visibility

3) Operational Verification

Confirms that funded work occurred in the real world.

  • Project reports tied to funded categories
  • Deliverables logged (services delivered, units built)
  • Vendor / partner documentation where relevant

4) Public Transparency Layer

Pushes verified data into live public dashboards so everyone sees the same truth.

  • Public tiles / panels on the LIVE page
  • Progress bars tied to specific goals
  • Audit-friendly history (no hidden versions)
Access Who can be verified

Verification is not about status — it’s about accountability. Nonprofits, charities, foundations, corporations, and community projects can all participate as long as they are willing to connect to verification systems and publish results.

Truth What happens when someone won’t verify

Refusing verification isn’t punished — it’s simply visible. If an entity chooses not to connect identity, finances, or outcomes, then their panel remains incomplete and their claims remain unconfirmed.

The market decides what that means. Service Human just makes the truth easier to see.

The end goal is simple: a world where good work rises to the top because it can be verified, not because it has the best marketing.
