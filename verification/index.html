Verification networks

Service Human is designed to operate inside trusted systems used by employers, foundations, giving platforms, and donors.

Candid / GuideStar

Helps confirm nonprofit identity, mission, and public-facing organizational information used by foundations, journalists, donors, and grantmakers.

Benevity

Corporate giving and matching infrastructure used by employers to verify nonprofit eligibility for employee donations and workplace giving programs.

Bonterra

Workplace giving and grants management infrastructure used to support nonprofit verification, donor pathways, and institutional giving workflows.

Blackbaud

Nonprofit fundraising and giving infrastructure used across philanthropic systems to support traceability, legitimacy, and compliance.

Platform mechanics vary by employer, provider, and system. These pathways support verification but do not replace public records, direct documentation, or Service Human’s own reporting.
